## **Tips for Recruitment:**

### **Target Audience:**
- Lecturers and tutors
- Academic advisors
- Student counselors
- Educational administrators
- Faculty with student support responsibilities

### **Recruitment Channels:**
1. **Email** - Use institutional directories
2. **Department meetings** - Brief presentation + handout
3. **Faculty WhatsApp groups** - Short message with links
4. **Professional networks** - LinkedIn, academic forums
5. **Colleague referrals** - Ask participants to share with peers

### **Response Rate Tips:**
- ✅ Emphasize time commitment (25-30 mins)
- ✅ Highlight anonymity and voluntary participation
- ✅ Mention contribution to educational technology
- ✅ Send reminder after 1 week
- ✅ Thank participants promptly

### **Sample Size Goals:**
- Minimum: 20-30 participants
- Ideal: 30-50 participants
- For statistical power in paired t-tests
